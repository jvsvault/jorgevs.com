##  CATALOG

For a long time now, I have been working on my own sound, my catalog. A sound I feel at home doing. My hope is make it available in the following months for music supervisors consideration, and as a way to properly introduce myself to projects that feel in sync with who I am at this point in time.

If you want a heads up when my catalog is ready, check my subscribe page.

In the meantime, you can see me working on one of my solo projects, VISE. VISE is a project I use as a playground to experiment the crossroads between classical, electronic and a bit of folklore sounds. I also look pretty in the videos, and I convinced a lot of cool people to participate in the project. An entertaining watch.
