## CONTACT

**I'm available for commissioned work.** Scores, additional music, sound design, and music for image in general. If you're a director, a producer or a music supervisor, this is the fastest way to reach me. I have also experience assisting composers, as I did for Olivier Arson in the soundtrack of Rodrigo Sorogoyen's "El Ser Querido", starring Javier Bardem and Victoria Luengo. I know my way around DAWs, MIDI, Sibelius, all those things that take valuable time and energy from a composer that they could be spending on well, making music.

As a composer, I'm a one-stop profile. I own the composition and the master, have the ability to work on anything on my own, so licensing takes one email.
