## Privacy Policy

This covers the newsletter subscription at [jorgevs.com/subscribe](/subscribe "email-link").

### Who holds the data

Jorge Viñals, on his own self-hosted server. No third party — no Mailchimp, no Substack, no ad network — has access to this list.

### What is stored

- Your email address
- Your name, if you provided one
- Which list(s) you chose to subscribe to
- The date and time you subscribed
- Basic open/click stats tied to campaigns, used only to see if a newsletter reached inboxes — not sold or shared

### How it works

You'll only receive mail when there's something real to send — no automated drip sequences.

### Unsubscribing or deletion

Every email includes an unsubscribe link. Clicking it removes you from that list immediately. To have your data fully deleted rather than just unsubscribed, email [hola@jorgevs.com](mailto:hola@jorgevs.com "email-link").
