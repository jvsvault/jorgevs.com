## COMPOSITION NOTES

I was a Conservatory kid that started making music from an early age, and later in life became a composer for others by chance.

Over the years, working across so many projects turned me into a Swiss Army knife of sorts, but in the process my own identity was buried. After taking time to perfect my craft and realign with who I am and who I want to become, I'm ready to take on commissioned work again. But only on projects where I can stay true to myself.

My current sound is fluid, bridging the space between acoustic and electronic. Sometimes it's harmonic and soft, others it's dissonant or noisy. It's always expressive and emotional, and it usually has a signature melancholic sound that many describe as a core element of my style.

The latest highlight in my work for film was being the assistant of composer Olivier Arson in Rodrigo Sorogoyen's feature film "El Ser Querido", starring Javier Bardem and Victoria Luengo.

Because of my origins in classical education and not long after making music with computers, one of my facets is the ability to work with MIDI, scores, audio, DAWs, and all those sort of things that take time from a composer that they could use well, making music. My engineering side also made me an operations animal, so I'm good at organizing anything else related to the process of making a soundtrack. Just in case you are in need of a figure who solves and puts down fires silently on a production.
